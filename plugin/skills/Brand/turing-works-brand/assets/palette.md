# Turing Works palette

| Role    | Name           | Hex     | RGB           | Use |
|---------|----------------|---------|---------------|-----|
| Ink     | Blueprint blue | #1B3A5C | 27, 58, 92    | All type, lines, the mark, chart lines. Also the background of an inverted surface |
| Surface | White          | #FFFFFF | 255, 255, 255 | The default surface everywhere — slides, docs, web, email, charts, social |
| Pen     | Vermilion      | #D9432A | 217, 67, 42   | The mark's overlaps, and at most one other thing per page |
| Guide   | Pale blueprint | #BFD3E6 | 191, 211, 230 | Grids, baselines, axes, rules. Never carries meaning |
| Muted   | Blue-grey      | #5C7189 | 92, 113, 137  | Secondary text, captions, table headers, axis labels |
| Paper   | Warm off-white | #F4F1EA | 244, 241, 234 | **Restricted.** Large-format physical print only, where pure white glares |

## Rules

- **White is the surface. Use it as much as possible.** If you are unsure which
  surface applies, it is white.
- Warm off-white is not a general-purpose background. It is not a card fill, not
  a panel, not a "softer white" for slides or web.
- When a surface must be dark it is ink `#1B3A5C` with white type and pale-blueprint
  guides — section breaks and covers only, never a page of body copy.
- Vermilion appears at most once per page apart from the mark.
- No black, no grey panels, no green, no purple, no gradients.
