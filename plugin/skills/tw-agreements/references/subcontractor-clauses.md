# Standard Clauses — Turing Works Subcontractor Agreements

These clauses are reproduced verbatim in every TW **subcontractor** agreement. Do not paraphrase, shorten, or restructure. Only the variables in `{braces}` change per engagement.

This file covers the **Subcontractor Agreement** branch only. For client engagement agreements, use `standard-clauses.md`. The Confidentiality clause is shared between both branches (see note in Section 7 below).

**Core framing (James's rules — do not get these wrong):**

- The agreement is between **the subcontractor's own legal entity and Turing Works** — never between the subcontractor and TW's end client.
- Frame the subcontractor in the recitals as **a consultant to TW, engaged for a defined period to assist on a specific project**.
- The subcontractor does **not** review the head (client) agreement; they only review and sign their own.
- Pronouns below use she/her to match the established template — adjust to match the actual subcontractor.

## Opening / Framing (template)

Further to our recent discussions, we, Turing Works ("we" or "TW"), are pleased to engage you to assist on a current client project. For the purpose of this agreement and its terms, this agreement is between Turing Works ("TW") and {subcontractor_entity} ("Consultant" or "you").

The Consultant is engaged as an independent consultant to TW, for a defined period and for the specific purpose of assisting TW on a single client project (the "Project"). The Consultant is engaged by TW only. This agreement does not create any contractual relationship between the Consultant and TW's client, and the Consultant will have no direct engagement with, or claim against, that client.

In this letter we outline the Services and Deliverables the Consultant will provide, together with the terms and conditions that govern this engagement.

Note: `{subcontractor_entity}` is the subcontractor's full legal entity name. Render `"Project"` defined-term in smart quotes.

---

## Section 1: Background

**1. Background**
TW has been engaged by its client to deliver a marketing and growth engagement (the "Head Engagement"). TW engages the Consultant to perform defined elements of the delivery under that Head Engagement. The Consultant is engaged for a defined period and for the specific purpose of the Project, and not as an employee, partner, or agent of TW.

The Consultant's obligations and liability under this agreement are intended to operate back-to-back with TW's obligations and liability to its client under the Head Engagement, so that responsibility for the Consultant's work flows through consistently (see Section 9, Liability).

## Section 8: Insurance

**8. Insurance**

- The Consultant must hold and maintain, at her own cost, current professional indemnity insurance and public liability insurance appropriate to the Services, for the term of this agreement and for a reasonable period afterwards
- The Consultant must provide TW with certificates of currency evidencing this cover on request
- If the Consultant fails to hold or evidence the required insurance, TW may suspend or terminate this agreement

Rationale (James): mandatory, and mainly protects the subcontractor — without it, exposure is effectively unlimited; insurance caps it. This clause already appears in TW's own agreements.

---

## Section 9: Liability

**9. Liability**

- The Consultant is liable to TW for loss or damage arising from the Services, including from any breach of this agreement or any negligent, wrongful, or defective performance
- The Consultant's obligations and liability under this agreement operate back-to-back with TW's obligations and liability to its client under the Head Engagement. Where TW incurs liability to its client arising from or connected with the Consultant's Services, the Consultant is liable to TW for that liability, and indemnifies TW against it, to the same extent
- The Consultant's liability under this agreement is not limited below TW's corresponding exposure to its client under the Head Engagement
- Nothing in this Section limits the insurance obligations in Section 8

**Flow-through rule (do not get this wrong):** keep the subcontractor's liability **at or above** TW's exposure to the client under the head agreement. If the subcontractor's liability is capped *lower* than TW's upstream exposure, TW stays liable to the client but cannot recover downstream.

**Do NOT proactively include a liability cap in the subcontractor's favour.** If the subcontractor asks for one, a cap to the project value is acceptable — *provided it stays at or above TW's upstream exposure*. Fallback wording (add only on request):

> The Consultant's total liability under this agreement is limited to the total fees payable to the Consultant under it, except that this limit will not apply to the extent it would reduce the Consultant's liability below TW's corresponding liability to its client under the Head Engagement.

## Section 11: Intellectual Property

**11. Intellectual Property**
The Consultant agrees that all work product, deliverables, materials, inventions, discoveries, designs, developments, and other items of any kind created, prepared, or produced by the Consultant in the course of performing the Services (collectively, "Work Product") shall be the sole and exclusive property of TW. The Consultant hereby assigns and agrees to assign to TW all right, title, and interest in and to such Work Product, including all associated intellectual property rights, so that TW may in turn meet its obligations to its client. The Consultant agrees to execute any documents and take any actions reasonably requested by TW to evidence or perfect TW's ownership of the Work Product.

For clarity, this does not prevent the Consultant from reusing her own underlying methods, frameworks, templates, or know-how developed during the course of providing the Services.

## Dependency — head (client) agreement

This gates the whole arrangement and lives in the **client agreement**, not the subcontract:

- The client (head) agreement must contain a **subcontracting-permission clause** — TW may engage subcontractors provided the client is informed. See `standard-clauses.md` › Subcontracting.
- Informing the client and obtaining their acknowledgement (a "sounds good" is sufficient) is the trigger; the subcontractor does not review the client agreement.
